## Executive Positioning

Mr. Nice Vape should compete as a Germany-first, compliance-led vape ecommerce brand: clear product navigation, strong category hubs, cautious regulated-industry language, transparent commercial terms, and answer-ready educational content.

The core organic goal is not only to rank product pages. The goal is to build a defensible topic entity around:

- Vape Shop Deutschland
- Vape Shop Koeln
- E-Zigarette / Vape kaufen
- E-Liquid online kaufen
- Einweg-Vapes Deutschland
- Coils, Pods, Kits, Akkus, Zubehoer
- Jugendschutz, TPD2, TabStG, DSGVO and safe purchasing guidance

Important limitation: this document uses modeled German-market demand tiers and a seed competitor set. Exact monthly search volume, keyword difficulty, PAA exports, and live SERP feature ownership must be validated with Google Keyword Planner, Google Search Console, Google Trends, Ahrefs/Semrush/Sistrix, and manual google.de SERP checks before final prioritization.

## Strategic Guardrails

- Do not make smoking cessation, health improvement, medical, or risk-reduction claims unless they are sourced, balanced, and reviewed by qualified expertise.
- Keep all commercial language 18+ and product-focused.
- Keep nicotine, TPD2, and youth-protection information factual and non-promotional.
- Avoid keyword stuffing, doorway location pages, fake reviews, fake expert bios, or fabricated certifications.
- Treat Google Merchant Center as high-risk because vape/e-cigarette products fall under restricted tobacco/nicotine policy areas. Free listings may still be rejected or limited.

## Structured Data Requirements

Implemented schema should be tested with Rich Results Test and Schema Markup Validator after every template change.

### Product JSON-LD Requirements

Must include:

- `@type: Product`
- `name`, `description`, `image`
- `sku`, `mpn`, and GTIN/EAN if available
- `brand`
- `offers.price`, `offers.priceCurrency: EUR`
- `offers.availability`
- `offers.itemCondition`
- `offers.shippingDetails`
- `offers.hasMerchantReturnPolicy`
- `aggregateRating` only if reviews are genuine and compliant
- `review` only if reviews are real, visible, and policy compliant

### FAQPage Requirements

Only mark up questions and answers visible on the page. Do not use FAQ schema for hidden, promotional, or misleading content.

### HowTo Requirements

Use only for genuine step-by-step instructional content. Do not use HowTo for regulated medical or safety claims.

### Organization / LocalBusiness

Use Organization sitewide. Use LocalBusiness only if a real physical customer-facing location exists and NAP is legally valid.

## AEO / AI Overview Playbook

For each guide page:

- Start major sections with a direct answer in 40-60 words.
- Use one clear entity per sentence when possible.
- Avoid vague pronouns in answer blocks.
- Add `Kurzantwort:` labels for snippet-ready paragraphs.
- Add tables for comparisons.
- Add ordered steps for processes.
- Add `Stand: YYYY-MM-DD` for legal and regulatory content.
- Cite official sources in legal/health topics.
- Keep commercial CTAs after the answer, not inside the answer.
